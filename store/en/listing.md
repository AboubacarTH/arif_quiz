## Full description  *(max 4000 characters)*

```
Answer, learn, and come back tomorrow.

ArifQuiz turns a quiz into a game you keep playing. Pick a topic, pick how you
want to play it, and see how far you get — alone, against your friends, or
against everyone.

SEVEN WAYS TO PLAY THE SAME QUIZ

• Classic — a timer on each question, scored as a percentage
• Survival — one wrong answer and the round is over
• Speed Round — enough time to read, not enough to hesitate
• Precision — +2 for a right answer, −1 for a wrong one, 0 if you pass
• Streak — answers in a row are worth more and more; one mistake resets it
• Time Attack — one clock for the whole round, and right answers extend it
• Jokers — three helping hands (50/50, hint, extra time), each costs a point

Every mode has its own XP bonus. The mode changes the question you ask
yourself, not just the score at the end.

A CHALLENGE EVERY DAY

A new daily challenge each morning, worth bonus XP and keeping your streak
alive. Miss it and the streak goes back to zero.

CHALLENGE YOUR FRIENDS — OR EVERYONE

Send a private challenge by code, announce one to your friends, or launch it to
everyone. Announced challenges live for 24 hours: take them while they last.
Add friends, compare scores on the leaderboard, and see what they have been
playing.

CLIMB THE JOURNEY MAP

Levels unlock one after another along an illustrated path. Earn stars, go
further, and watch the landscape change as you climb.

TRACK EVERYTHING

Levels and XP, accuracy, quizzes played, current and best streak, achievements
to unlock, and a full history of your rounds with the correct answer and an
explanation for every question you got wrong.

FOUR LANGUAGES, INCLUDING THE QUESTIONS

English, French, Arabic and Spanish — and the language applies to the quiz
content too, not just the buttons. Arabic is fully right-to-left.

FREE TO PLAY

Play for free with ads, or go Premium for unlimited ad-free rounds.

Made for anyone who would rather learn something than scroll.
```
